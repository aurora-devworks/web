ARCANE TRIAD - RULEBOOK

Master directional values and the 3x3 tactical grid.

1. The 3x3 Grid: Players take turns placing 1 card from their 5-card hand onto any empty slot on the 3x3 board.

2. Directional Stats: Each card has 4 directional numbers (Top, Right, Bottom, Left) ranging from 1 to 10 (represented by 1-9 and A for 10).

3. Flipping Cards: When you place a card adjacent to an opponent's card, compare your touching side against their touching side. If your number is strictly greater, you flip their card to your color!

4. Winning the Match: Once all 9 grid spaces are filled, the player with the most cards of their color on the board wins the match and earns gold and booster crates!
